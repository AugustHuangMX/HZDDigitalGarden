#Econ/Micro 

In the very basic economic course, in most of the times, we just need to make sure that:

$$
\text{Supply} = \text{Demand}
$$

And then we are done. But in a more advanced way, we often face a situation that there are multiple markets, and multiple goods, making the problem non-linear.  For example, there are $L$ goods, and $N$ consumers, each consumer may have a nonlinear utility function (for example, a cobb-douglas utility function). In this case, before solving the equilibrium, we need to first prove that there exists an equilibrium. Thus we need to introduce **Kakutani's Fixed Point Theorem**.
