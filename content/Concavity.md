To determine whether a function is concave or not, we could use two aspects:

1. Concavity: In Economic context, it usually refers to *diminishing [[Marginal Utility]]*.

If there is only one good in the economy, simply calculate $u''(x)$. If $u''(x) < 0$, then $u(\cdot)$ is concave.


2. Quasi-Concavity: (拟凹性): It refers to the "Love for variety". For any $x$ and $y$, if the utility function satisfies:

$$
u(\alpha x + (1-\alpha) y) \geq \min \{u(x),u(y)\}  \quad \forall \alpha \in [0,1]
$$

Then we say $u(\cdot)$ is quasi-concave.
