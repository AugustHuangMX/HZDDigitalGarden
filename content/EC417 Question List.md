## Lecture 8

- What does a stationary distribution mean?
	- It normally denotes to $g(a,y)$. If given policy function $a'(a,y)$ and transition probability $p_{jj'}$, if $g(a,y)$ remains the same overtime, then we say $g(a,y)$ is a stationary distribution. $g$ simply means the fraction of agents holding asset level $a$ and income level $y$.
