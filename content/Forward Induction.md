#GameTheory 

Unlike the [[Backward Induction]], the [[Forward Induction]] would start from the beginning of the sequential game, and at each history $h$, it would take the previous actions (history) into consideration to deduce the players' types and strategies.

While the [[Backward Induction]] focuses on the optimal actions at each subgame, the [[Forward Induction]] emphasizes the interpretation of actions taken earlier in the game.
