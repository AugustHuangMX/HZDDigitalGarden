#Econ/Macro 

## Model 

The main equations for the heterogeneous one is super similar to the normal [[New Keynesian Model]]. But we add a [[Wage Phillips Curve]].

The four equations:

1. [[New Keynesian Phillips Curve]]

$$\pi_ {t} = \kappa_ {p} \tilde {y} _ {t} + \Lambda \tilde {\omega} _ {t} + \beta \mathbb {E} _ {t} (\pi_ {t + 1})$$

2. [[Dynamic IS Equation]]

$$\tilde {y} _ {t} = \mathbb {E} _ {t} \left(\tilde {y} _ {t + 1}\right) - \frac {1}{\sigma} \left(i _ {t} - \mathbb {E} _ {t} \left(\pi_ {t + 1}\right) - r _ {t} ^ {n}\right)$$
3. [[Wage Phillips Curve]]

$$\pi_ {t} ^ {w} = \beta \mathbb {E} _ {t} \left(\pi_ {t + 1} ^ {w}\right) - \kappa_ {w} \varphi \widehat {u} _ {t} ^ {w}$$

4. [[Monetary Policy Rule]]

$$i _ {t} = \rho + \phi_ {\pi} \pi_ {t} + \phi_ {y} \tilde {y} _ {t} + v _ {t}$$
