#Econ 

Unlike **direct** utility function, the [[Indirect Utility Function]] takes *price* as the input.

本质上体现的是，我们研究不在意人具体消费了什么，而是通过改变 $p$ or $w$ to investigate how they would affect workers' utility.

We usually formed by $V(p,y)$ , which is also: $V(p,y) = \max_{x} U(x)$ subject to $p \cdot x \leq y$ , and then we could use [[Lagrangian Multiplier]] to solve the maximization problem.

and the solution is then [[Marshallian Demand]].
