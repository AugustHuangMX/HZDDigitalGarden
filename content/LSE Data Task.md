Methodological Note:

This analysis uses GVA and capital stock at current prices 
(CP_MEUR) rather than constant prices. This may lead to 
overestimation of TFP growth levels due to nominal effects. 
However, the relative patterns across countries, sectors, 
and time remain valid for comparative analysis.

The data shows expected patterns including negative growth 
during the 2008-2009 financial crisis, supporting the 
validity of our calculations despite the use of nominal values.
