#GameTheory 

The [[Maximally Greedy Action Plan]] is a concept in the [[Game Theory]], It normally happens in the extensive-form bargaining games.

Suppose we are in a pie-divided game, there are $N = 2$ players, and the [[Maximally Greedy Action Plan]] means that, when player $j$ is making proposal, he would only propose $(1,0)$, and he would reject all the proposals made by player $-j$.

The [[Maximally Greedy Action Plan]] has some properties, for example, we can prove that, any action plan is a [[Best Response]] against the [[Maximally Greedy Action Plan]], such that any action plan is [[Rationalizable]].
