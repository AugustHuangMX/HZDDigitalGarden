the key difference between [[NK-DSGE]] model and [[Real Business Cycle]] is that it assumes the *price* is sticky.

Both models share the same backbone: household utility maximization, firm's production and firms' profit maximization, and market clearing.

Unlike [[Real Business Cycle]] setting, the price of the goods can't adjust freely, so it makes it more difficult to clear the market. A typical example is the [[Calvo Price]]
