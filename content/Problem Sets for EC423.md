# Problem Set 1

1. Consider the one-period choice problem of a worker endowed with unearned income $y$ and $T$ hours of time. The worker faces wage $w$. The worker's preferences can be described by the utility function
$$U(c, l) = c^\alpha l$$
where $c$ represents consumption of a composite good with a fixed price of 1 and $l$ represents leisure.



(a) Derive the worker's labor supply function and the uncompensated and compensated wage elasticities. Compute the desired hours of work $h^*$ for the case where $T = 80, y = 0, \alpha = 1$.

(b) Consider a worker supplying the desired hours of work $h^*$. The firm wants the worker to supply overtime hours $h'$ at wage $w' > w$ in addition to the first $h^*$ hours remunerated at wage $w$. Derive the overtime labor supply elasticity at $h' = 0$. How does this elasticity relate to the elasticities of the standard hours supply function in (a)? Think of the case where $w'$ is close to $w$.

(c) Using the parameters in (a), derive the overtime premium $w'/w$ necessary to induce the worker to supply $h' = h^*/4$ hours of overtime.
