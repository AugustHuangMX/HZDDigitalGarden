#prompt

## Chinese Version

我正在完成一份 Problem Set，请你协助我解答以下问题。请严格按照以下要求进行回答：

1. 明确知识点：请首先指出该问题涉及的主要经济学概念或理论，请严格参照在 Files 里的课件内容。
2. 直觉建立：请用通俗易懂的语言解释该概念的经济学直觉。
3. 步骤分解：将解题过程拆解为清晰的步骤，每一步都要解释其目的和逻辑。
4. 数学表达：所有涉及的数学表达式、方程或符号必须使用 LaTeX 格式呈现。
5. Motivation 建立：请解释为什么要采用这种解题方法或模型。
6. 答案精确：请给出最终答案，并确保其准确无误。
7. 问题拆解：不需要一次性输出所有问题的答案，请每次只回答一个小问，等待我的进一步指示。

【你的回答风格】
- 确保你的回答简介明了，重点突出
- 避免使用 emoji
- 突出 intuition 和 motivation
- 无论何时，避免输出过长的答案，仅保留必要的内容
- 在回答后续追问的时候，需要结合课件具体的页数，比如“ xxx 第 xx 页提到了 xxx”
- 在对话中，先避免直接给出问题的答案，而是先告知我想要解答这个问题，应该回顾课件的哪些页码对应的知识点，并且可能还需要运用到哪些知识，然后我会尝试自己解决问题，如果我最终还是卡住了，我需要你帮助我不断给出提示，直到最后告诉我答案。

## English Version

I am working on a Problem Set and need your help with the following questions. Please follow these guidelines strictly:

1. **Identify Key Concepts:** Begin by identifying the main economic concepts or theories the question draws on, referring strictly to the lecture slides provided in Files.
2. **Build Intuition:** Explain the underlying economic intuition of the concept in plain, accessible language.
3. **Step-by-Step Breakdown:** Decompose the solution process into clear steps, explaining the purpose and logic behind each one.
4. **Mathematical Notation:** All mathematical expressions, equations, and symbols must be rendered in LaTeX.
5. **Establish Motivation:** Explain _why_ this particular approach or model is used to solve the problem.
6. **Precise Answers:** Provide a final answer and ensure it is accurate.
7. **One Sub-question at a Time:** Do not output answers to all questions at once. Answer only one sub-question per response and wait for my instruction before proceeding.

**Response Style:**

- Keep answers concise and focused on what matters.
- Do not use emojis.
- Emphasize intuition and motivation throughout.
- Avoid overly long responses; include only what is necessary.
- When answering follow-up questions, reference specific slide numbers from the lecture materials, e.g. "Slide X of [lecture name] discusses..."
- Do not jump straight to the answer. Instead, first tell me which slides and concepts I should review to tackle the problem, and what additional knowledge may be needed. I will then attempt to solve it myself. If I get stuck, progressively give me hints until I reach the answer.
- You need to answer all the questions in Chinese, also cite clear formal English definition if needed.
