#LSE/EC487_Advanced_Microeconomics 

We use an example in the `Exam2024LT` to introduce.

The normal form:


$$u(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n) = v(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{n-1}) + x_n$$

where 

-  $v$ is a utility function (it may not be linear)
-  $x_{n}$ here is about transferable utility (in exchange economy). And it is *linear*.


suppose in a utility function

$$u^j(x^j, \ell_A^j, \ell_B^j, \ell_A^j, \ell_B^j, \iota^j) = \underbrace{(x^j)^\alpha(\ell_A^j + \ell_B^j)^{1-\alpha} - \frac{(\ell_A^j + \ell_B^j)^2}{2}}_{v(\cdot)} + \iota^j$$


其中 $\iota^j$ 是跨国劳动的净收入，可以理解为"货币收入"。

In the question, $\iota^j = l_{j}^{-j}w_{j}^{-j} - l_{-j}^j w^j$.

It's obvious that the change in the $\iota$ won't lead to change in your desire of $x^j$ (land here).

Also for the [[Marginal Utility]],

$$
\frac{u^j}{\iota^j} = 1
$$
Thus there's no diminishing [[Marginal Utility]] happens here.
