Before introducing [[Walrasian Equilibrium]], we need to define a concept which is **Excess Demand**.

$$
z(p) = \sum_{j=1}^{N} x^j(p,w^j(p)) - \sum_{j=1}^{N} e^j
$$

where $e$ is the [[Endowment]]. Excess demand is positive (negative) if consumers demand greater (less) quantity of a good than they supply via their [[Endowment]]. Thus in equilibrium, we must have $z(p) = 0$.


The intuition of this concept is that imagine there exists a *Walrasian Auctioneer*, who announces a price vector $p \in \mathbb{R}_{+}^L$. All buyers and sellers in this exchange economy are "price takers". If in the economy under this price, there are consumers that want to buy but could not, i.e., $z(p) > 0$, then the auctioneer would increase the price of this good to reduce demand, and may decrease other goods' price to increase demand. This process continues until the market clears, i.e., $z(p) = 0$.
