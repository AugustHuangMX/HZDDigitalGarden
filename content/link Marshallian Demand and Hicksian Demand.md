#LSE/EC423_Labour 

We know that the [[Hicksian Demand]] is the minimum value function:

$$
e(p,u) = \min_{x} p \cdot x \quad \text{s.t.} \quad u(x)>u
$$

Note that we could not really observe [[Hicksian Demand]], because in applied research, the utility is unmeasurable. Such that we could use [[Slutsky Equation]] to solve this problem.
