---
title: p-norm
---
For vector $a = (a_{1},\dots,a_{k})'$ , some classic p-norms are:

$$
||a||_{1} = \sum^k_{j=1} |a_{j}|
$$
$$
||a||_{2} = \left( \sum^k_{j=1} a_{j}^2 \right)^{1/2}
$$

$$
||a||_{\infty} = \max_{1\leq j\leq k} |a_{j}|
$$
$$
||a||_{0} = \sum^k_{j=1} \mathbb{I}\{a_{j} \neq 0\}
$$
